# Customer Activation & License Transfer Guide

## 1. How Customers Activate
1. Launch **NovaCut AI Video Studio**.
2. If unlicensed, the **Customer Activation Modal** appears automatically.
3. Paste your license key (format: `NVST-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX`).
4. Click **Activate License on This PC**:
   - The application securely binds this installation to your machine's hardware ID.
   - An offline cryptographic lease (valid for 7 days without an internet connection) is stored locally.
   - All professional features (4K export, AI tools, bulk create, anti-copyright shield) unlock immediately.

---

## 2. Moving Your License to a New Computer
If you get a new PC or want to transfer your license:
1. On your current computer, click **Pro License** in the top navigation bar.
2. The **Active License Installation** dialog will open.
3. Click **Deactivate & Release Slot**.
4. Install NovaCut on your new computer and enter the same license key to activate it.

---

## 3. Offline Mode & Re-Verification
- NovaCut operates completely offline using cryptographically signed lease tokens.
- When an internet connection is available, the application automatically performs a lightweight background heartbeat to re-verify license status and extend your offline lease window.
