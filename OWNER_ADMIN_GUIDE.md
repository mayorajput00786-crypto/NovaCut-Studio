# Owner & Administrator Operations Guide

## 1. Owner Entitlement Summary
As the application owner, you receive permanent, lifetime access to all features:
- **Zero Seat Consumption**: Personal devices do not use customer license seats.
- **Permanent Entitlement**: Never expires.
- **Admin Hub Access**: Available across all your authorized computers.
- **Complete Control**: Generate single or batch licenses, revoke or suspend access, manage hardware device bindings, and view audit trails.

---

## 2. Generating Customer Licenses
1. Open the application and click **Owner Admin** in the top navigation bar.
2. Select the **Key Generator** tab.
3. Choose the plan duration:
   - **Pro Monthly**: 30-day access.
   - **Annual VIP**: 365-day access.
   - **Lifetime Enterprise**: Permanent customer access.
   - **7-Day Fast Trial**: Ideal for evaluations.
   - **Custom Days**: Set any specific duration (e.g. 90 days).
4. Configure the **Hardware Seat Limit** (e.g., 1 PC, 2 PCs, 5 PCs).
5. Specify customer name, email, and internal order notes.
6. Choose the **Batch Quantity** (1 to 50 keys).
7. Click **Generate License Key(s)**:
   - Copy individual keys directly to your clipboard.
   - Click **Download CSV** to export the entire batch for distribution.

---

## 3. Managing Hardware Device Slots
When a customer purchases a new computer or reinstalls Windows:
- Find their license in the **License Directory**.
- Click **View Hardware** to see the machine ID, OS, and activation date.
- Click the **Laptop Icon (Reset Devices)** to clear their active hardware bindings and free all seats immediately.

---

## 4. Revocation & Suspension
- **Suspend**: Temporarily pauses a customer's ability to export or use AI features.
- **Revoke**: Permanently terminates access and immediately clears connected devices.
- **Extend (+30d)**: Adds 30 days to the license expiration date with 1 click.

---

## 5. Running Security Tests
Click the **Security Tests** tab in the Admin Hub at any time to run the live 18-point verification suite.
